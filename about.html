Our Story

A Legacy of Faith

The Ahli Hadith Movement in Jammu & Kashmir began in 1858 A.D. as a reform movement dedicated to propagating the teachings of the Quran and authentic Hadith in their purest form.

The foundation of the movement was laid by Moulana Sayyid Hussain Shah during the Dogra rule of Maharaja Ranbir Singh. He challenged polytheistic practices, religious innovations and the prevailing culture of Pir-Mureedi.

Despite facing strong opposition, the movement continued to grow through the efforts of dedicated scholars and reformers. In 1923 A.D., it was formally named Anjuman-e-Ahle Hadith Kashmir.

In 1940 A.D., the organization was rechristened as Jamiat Ahle-Hadith Jammu & Kashmir. On 20 September 1958, it was formally registered as a society by the Government.

Our Journey

Historical Timeline

From a reform movement in 1858 to a major Islamic organization serving Jammu & Kashmir.

Foundation of the Movement

Moulana Sayyid Hussain Shah laid the foundation of the Ahli Hadith Movement in Kashmir and called people towards the Quran and authentic Sunnah.

1923

Anjuman-e-Ahle Hadith Kashmir

The movement was formally organized and named Anjuman-e-Ahle Hadith Kashmir.

1940

On 20 September 1958, Jamiat Ahle-Hadith Jammu & Kashmir was formally registered as a society.

Today

Dawah, Education & Welfare

Today, Jamiat Ahle-Hadith Jammu & Kashmir continues its work in Dawah, education, social welfare and humanitarian service from its headquarters at Jamiat Manzil, Barbar Shah, Srinagar.

Serving Humanity

Healthcare & Welfare

Jamiat also serves society through medical, humanitarian and social-welfare initiatives.

TMU Diagnostic Centre

Affordable diagnostic and medical services for people without discrimination.

Collection Centres

Healthcare collection centres serving people in different locations of the region.

Dialysis Centre

Dialysis services established on a no-profit, no-loss basis.

Orphan Support

Assistance and care for orphaned children and vulnerable families.

Widow Assistance

Welfare support for widows and families in need.

Disaster Relief

Support for victims of natural calamities and other disasters.
