The core mission and vision of Jamiat Ahle Hadees J&K
Our Mission

What We Stand For

Jamiat Ahle Hadees J&K is guided by a clear vision rooted in the Quran and authentic Sunnah, serving the Muslim community of the region.

Propagation of Tawheed

Spreading pure monotheism and the authentic teachings of Islam according to the understanding of the Salaf-us-Saliheen.

Quran & Sunnah

Preaching and propagating the Quran and authentic Hadith in their purest form while encouraging adherence to authentic Islamic teachings.

Islamic & Arabic Sciences

Promoting Islamic and Arabic sciences and supporting institutions dedicated to religious learning and scholarship.

Modern Education

Encouraging modern education and strengthening institutions that contribute to academic excellence throughout Jammu & Kashmir.

Universal Call of Islam

Conveying the universal message of Islam to Muslims and non-Muslims through peaceful, constructive and meaningful engagement.

Brotherhood & Unity

Strengthening unity, cooperation and mutual understanding among Muslims while promoting Islamic and human brotherhood.

Protection of Endowments

Protecting Muslim endowments and waqf properties and ensuring their proper management for the benefit of the community.

Heritage Preservation

Preserving the educational, cultural and civilizational heritage of the Muslim community and future generations.

Social Welfare

Supporting orphans, widows, underprivileged families and victims of natural calamities through charitable and welfare initiatives.

Healthcare Services

Serving society through medical centres, diagnostic facilities and healthcare projects accessible to all without discrimination.
