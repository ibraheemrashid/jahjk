The core mission and vision of Jamiat Ahle Hadees J&K
Our Mission

What We Stand For

Jamiat Ahle Hadees J&K is guided by a clear vision rooted in the Quran and authentic Sunnah, serving the Muslim community of the region.

Propagation of Tawheed

Spreading pure monotheism and the authentic teachings of Islam as understood by the companions and the Salaf-us-Saliheen.

Islamic Education

Establishing and supporting schools, colleges and madarsas that provide quality religious and modern education across J&K.

Community Unity

Fostering unity, brotherhood and cooperation among the Muslim community of Jammu & Kashmir.

Masjid Development

Building, maintaining and registering mosques as centres of worship, learning and community gathering.

Knowledge Dissemination

Publishing Islamic literature including books, journals and newspapers to spread authentic religious knowledge.

Religious Guidance

Providing authentic Fatawa and religious guidance through a qualified and trusted Ifta Board.

Social Welfare

Supporting underprivileged members of the Muslim community through charitable initiatives and welfare programmes.

Waqf Preservation

Protecting Islamic endowment properties through proper registration and management under Waqf-i-Tanzeem.
