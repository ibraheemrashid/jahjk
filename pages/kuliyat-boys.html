About the Institution

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Baneen

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Baneen is a premier Islamic college for men established under the SMERT framework. It provides rigorous academic training in the classical Islamic sciences including Quran, Hadees, Fiqh, Aqeedah and Arabic language.

Graduates of the college go on to serve as Imams, scholars, teachers and community leaders across Jammu & Kashmir, spreading authentic Islamic knowledge.

Curriculum

Quran Memorisation, Hadees Sciences, Islamic Fiqh, Aqeedah, Arabic Language and Grammar.

Admissions

Applications open annually. Candidates must have completed basic Islamic education and passed the entrance evaluation.

Facilities

Dedicated library, lecture halls, residential facilities and a mosque on campus for regular prayers and Islamic programmes.

Graduation

Annual graduation ceremony upon completion of the full programme, with certificates recognised across J&K and beyond.
