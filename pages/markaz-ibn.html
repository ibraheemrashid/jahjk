Islamic Centre

Markaz Ibn Masood

Named after the noble companion Abdullah ibn Masood (رضي الله عنه), this centre serves as a hub for Islamic education, da'wah and community outreach.

📖 Islamic Classes

Regular classes in Quran, Hadees, Aqeedah, Fiqh and Arabic for students of all ages and backgrounds.

🎤 Lectures & Seminars

Hosting regular Islamic lectures, seminars and workshops by local and visiting scholars on contemporary Islamic issues.

📚 Library

A well-stocked library of Islamic books, journals and references available to the community and researchers.

🤝 Da'wah Activities

Active da'wah programme including outreach to new Muslims, distribution of Islamic materials and community engagement.
