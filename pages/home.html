Who We Are

Built on Knowledge & Community

A trusted Islamic organisation serving the people of Jammu & Kashmir through education, guidance, and community welfare.

Islamic Education

Supporting Madrasas, schools, and colleges that provide authentic Islamic and modern education throughout J&K.

Fatwa & Guidance

Connecting the community with qualified scholars for authentic Fatawa on worship, family, and daily life.

Masjid Welfare

Registering and supporting Masajid and Islamic properties under the Waqf-i-Tanzeem framework.

Publications

Producing Islamic literature including Meeqat, scholarly books, and the Weekly Muslim newspaper.
