Sunday & Vocational Classes

For those who cannot attend full-time religious institutions, our Sunday and vocational classes provide accessible Islamic education alongside practical skills.

Sunday Islamic Classes

Weekly Islamic education covering Quran, basic Fiqh, Aqeedah and Arabic for adults and children.

Vocational Training

Skill development courses in computing, tailoring, calligraphy and other practical fields for community members.

Youth Programme

Dedicated programme for Muslim youth combining Islamic character development with leadership and community skills.

Certificates

Certificates awarded upon completion of each course, recognised within the Jamiat network of institutions.
