Women's Centre

Markaz Aisha

Named after Aisha bint Abi Bakr (رضي الله عنها) — Mother of the Believers — this centre is dedicated to the Islamic education and empowerment of Muslim women.

Women's Quran Classes

Dedicated Quran recitation, Tajweed and Hifz classes for women in a comfortable, women-only setting.

Islamic Studies

Courses in Islamic jurisprudence relevant to women's daily life, family matters and Islamic etiquette.

Counselling

Islamic counselling and guidance for Muslim women on matters of family, marriage, parenting and community life.

Talks & Programmes

Regular talks, workshops and Islamic programmes specifically designed for the needs of Muslim women and young girls.
