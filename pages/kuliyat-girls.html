About the Institution

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Banat

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Banat is a premier Islamic college for women, providing a comprehensive education in the authentic Islamic sciences in a safe and dedicated learning environment.

The college empowers Muslim women to become knowledgeable scholars, teachers and community leaders who contribute to the propagation of authentic Islamic knowledge within their families and communities.

Curriculum

Quran Memorisation (Hifz), Hadees, Fiqh, Aqeedah, Tafseer, Arabic Language and Islamic History.

Admissions

Open to Muslim women who have completed secondary education. Entrance evaluation required for all applicants.

Facilities

Women-only campus with dedicated library, lecture halls, and facilities designed for a focused learning environment.

Graduation

Annual graduation ceremony. Graduates receive certificates and are encouraged to serve as teachers and mentors.
