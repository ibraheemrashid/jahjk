About Us
Our history, mission and commitment to the Muslim community
Our Story

A Legacy of Faith

Jamiat Ahle Hadees Jammu & Kashmir is a distinguished Islamic organisation dedicated to propagating the teachings of the Quran and authentic Hadees among the Muslim community of the region.

Founded on the principles of adhering to the Quran and Sunnah as understood by the Salaf-us-Saliheen, we have established a comprehensive network of educational and religious institutions across Jammu & Kashmir.

Over the decades, we have grown into one of the most influential Islamic organisations in the region, with hundreds of affiliated mosques, schools, colleges and madarsas serving tens of thousands of Muslims.

About the Institution

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Baneen

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Baneen is a premier Islamic college for men established under the SMERT framework. It provides rigorous academic training in the classical Islamic sciences including Quran, Hadees, Fiqh, Aqeedah and Arabic language.

Graduates of the college go on to serve as Imams, scholars, teachers and community leaders across Jammu & Kashmir, spreading authentic Islamic knowledge.

Curriculum

Quran Memorisation, Hadees Sciences, Islamic Fiqh, Aqeedah, Arabic Language and Grammar.

Admissions

Applications open annually. Candidates must have completed basic Islamic education and passed the entrance evaluation.

Facilities

Dedicated library, lecture halls, residential facilities and a mosque on campus for regular prayers and Islamic programmes.

Graduation

Annual graduation ceremony upon completion of the full programme, with certificates recognised across J&K and beyond.

About the Institution

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Banat

Al Kulyat us Salafia Lil Banat is a premier Islamic college for women, providing a comprehensive education in the authentic Islamic sciences in a safe and dedicated learning environment.

The college empowers Muslim women to become knowledgeable scholars, teachers and community leaders who contribute to the propagation of authentic Islamic knowledge within their families and communities.

Graduation
