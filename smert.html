Our Structure

Aims & Goals

Jamiat Ahle Hadees J&K, is committed to a comprehensive vision for the Muslim community of the region.

Propagation of Quran & Sunnah

Spreading the authentic teachings of Islam as understood by the Salaf-us-Saliheen through education and da'wah.

Educational Excellence

Establishing and maintaining quality educational institutions that combine Islamic and modern education.

Community Welfare

Providing social welfare, charitable services and support to the Muslim community of J&K.

Waqf Management

Registering and managing Islamic endowment properties to preserve community assets for future generations.

Islamic Publications

Publishing books, periodicals and educational materials to spread Islamic knowledge and scholarship.

Fatwa & Guidance

Providing authentic Islamic rulings through a qualified Ifta Board to guide the community on religious matters.

Trust Structure

SMERT operates under a structured hierarchy ensuring accountability, transparency and adherence to Islamic principles in all activities. The trust is governed by a board of trustees comprising senior scholars and community leaders.
